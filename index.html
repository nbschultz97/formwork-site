SHEET A-100 · SOUND FAMILIAR?
Sound Familiar?
We've all seen what happens next.
The Spec Conflict

The structural notes call for Type V cement. Page 187 of the specs says Type II. Your estimator catches it — or they don't. If they don't, you're rebidding concrete three days after bid day while the owner wonders if you actually read the documents.

The Buried Requirement

Testing and inspection requirements are scattered across Division 01 and buried in individual spec sections. Your junior estimator misses the special inspections on page 243. You find out when the owner rejects your first submittal.

The Scope Gap

The mechanical drawings show a 6-inch main. The plumbing spec calls out 4-inch. Is it a drafting error from the last revision, or an intentional redesign that didn't make it to the final set? Either way, your bid is due Friday.

These aren't hypotheticals. They're last Tuesday.
Introducing Formwork
WHAT IF YOUR DOCUMENTS COULD TALK TO EACH OTHER?

Formwork reads every page of your bid package — specs, drawings, addenda — and cross-references them the way your best estimator would. It catches the conflict between page 187 and the structural notes. It flags the testing requirements buried in Division 01. It spots the pipe size discrepancy before your team even opens the mechanical drawings.

Not because it's smarter than your people. Because it never skips a page.
