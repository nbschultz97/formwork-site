FAQ

Straight answers.

How accurate is it?

Every quantity traces back to a sheet and a markup you can audit. Every clause and fee points to its page. Machines do the counting; an estimator checks it and signs it. If a number moves, you can see why.

Who signs the number?

A construction professional reviews and signs every deliverable that leaves Formwork. No black boxes, no unsigned output.

How fast is turnaround?

Gap check within 24 hours of receiving the set. Quantities and the full estimate before your pre-bid. We confirm the date the day the set lands, and tell you up front if the timeline is tight.

What does it cost?

The Gap Check is $0. A Bid Package starts at $750 per bid. Bid Desk starts at $3K per month for a monthly bid allotment. Owner-side work is quoted per project.

Which trades do you cover?

All of them: sitework, concrete, steel, framing and drywall, doors and hardware, finishes, fire protection, plumbing, mechanical, electrical, low voltage. GCs get the whole set; subs get their scope.

What happens when addenda drop?

We track addenda on every bid we have. Affected quantities are re-run and you get a short delta memo: what changed, where, and what it does to the number.

Do you work in our estimate template?

Yes. Send your template and we deliver in it. No template? You get a clean CSI-division estimate that drops into most systems.

Do we have to install anything?

No. You get files: marked-up drawings, the estimate, the RFI memo. Nothing to log into.
